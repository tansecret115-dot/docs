Preset templates are the fastest way to get started with KoboldCPP Colab Modded. Each template automatically configures the correct model URL, context size, quantization format, and multimodal projector (mmproj) file when one is needed — you do not need to touch any other setting. Simply select a template from the **Template** dropdown at the top of the notebook cell and click the play button to launch.

<Info>
  Named preset templates are active **only when no loader checkbox is ticked**. If you enable `LoadTextModel`, `LoadImgModel`, `LoadVisionMMProjector`, or any other loader checkbox, those manual selections override the chosen template completely.
</Info>

<Tip>
  The template selected by default when you first open the notebook is **GLM-4.7-Flash-REAP (MoE, 23B-A3B, Q3\_K\_S, 10.3GB)** — a fast mixture-of-experts model that fits comfortably within Colab's free-tier VRAM.
</Tip>

## Large / Mixture-of-Experts

These models use MoE architecture or are simply large, offering higher capability at the cost of slightly more VRAM or slower inference. Most still fit within Colab's free T4 GPU.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="GLM-4.7-Flash (30B, Q2_K, 11.3 GB)">
    **Context:** 6144 · **Quant:** Q2\_K · **Architecture:** MoE (30B-A3B)

    THUDM's GLM-4.7-Flash model in a highly compressed Q2\_K quantisation. Fast responses with a moderate context window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="GLM-4.7-Flash-REAP (MoE, 23B-A3B, Q3_K_S, 10.3 GB) — Default">
    **Context:** 10240 · **Quant:** Q3\_K\_S · **Architecture:** MoE (23B-A3B)

    The REAP variant of GLM-4.7-Flash. This is the **default selected template** when you open the notebook. Better quality than the base Q2\_K version while still fitting in free-tier VRAM.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Gemma4 12B (Q4_K_M, 7.12 GB)">
    **Context:** 16384 · **Quant:** Q4\_K\_M

    Google's Gemma 4 12B in a well-balanced Q4\_K\_M quantisation. Good quality-to-size ratio for a dense 12B model.
