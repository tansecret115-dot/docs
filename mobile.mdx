---
title: "Run KoboldCPP Colab Modded on Your Phone or Tablet"
sidebarTitle: "Mobile"
description: "Run KoboldCPP Colab Modded from your phone or tablet by enabling the audio keep-alive cell to prevent session timeouts on mobile browsers."
---

Mobile browsers aggressively throttle background tabs to save battery and memory, which means Google Colab can silently shut down your runtime while you are reading a response or have switched to another app. To prevent this, KoboldCPP Colab Modded includes a dedicated keep-alive cell that plays a silent audio loop, signalling to the browser that the tab is actively in use. Running this cell before you launch your model is all it takes to keep your mobile session stable.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the notebook in your mobile browser or the Colab app">
    Navigate to the [KoboldCPP Colab Modded notebook](https://colab.research.google.com/github/tansecret115-dot/KoboldCPP-Colab-Modded/blob/main/colab.ipynb) in your mobile browser (Chrome, Safari, or Firefox) or in the official Google Colab mobile app. Sign in with your Google account if prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the keep-alive cell">
    At the top of the notebook, find the cell labelled **"Tap this if you play on Mobile"** and press its play button. The cell starts a silent audio loop designed to keep the browser tab active and prevent the runtime from being throttled or killed by the operating system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the audio is playing">
    The cell plays a silent audio file, so you will not hear any sound. Some browsers require an explicit user interaction before allowing audio playback — if the loop does not start on its own, tap the audio player control that appears in the cell output to start it manually. A playback indicator in your browser tab or status bar confirms it is running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue with the normal quickstart steps">
    With the keep-alive loop running, proceed exactly as you would on a desktop: select a preset template in the model cell, press play, wait for the model to download and start, then copy the Cloudflare URL from the output and open it to begin your session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Keep your screen awake while the model is downloading and starting up. On most phones you can prevent auto-lock by adjusting your screen timeout setting or using a screen-awake app. Once the model is fully loaded and you have the Cloudflare URL, the audio keep-alive handles background protection.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The Cloudflare tunnel URL that appears in the cell output works on any device — you can start a session on your phone and then open the same URL on a tablet or desktop to continue right where you left off. The URL is tied to the Colab runtime, not to the device you used to launch it.
</Note>
